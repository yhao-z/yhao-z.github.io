Getting to know Yinghao Zhang

> about_me

I am Yinghao Zhang, a Ph.D. candidate at Harbin Institute of Technology, specializing in inverse problems in medical imaging. My primary research focuses on undersampled MRI image reconstruction, leveraging compressed sensing, deep learning, and tensor theory. The methodologies I develop are generalizable to a broader range of ill-posed problems, including image restoration and super-resolution. With a solid mathematical foundation and an in-depth research approach, I am dedicated to advancing computational imaging techniques for clinical applications.
