Getting to know Yinghao Zhang

I am Yinghao Zhang, a Ph.D. candidate at Harbin Institute of Technology, focusing on medical image reconstruction and tensor completion. My research interest is primarily in developing advanced deep learning methods for magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) reconstruction.

Research Interests

  • Medical Image Reconstruction: Focusing on dynamic MRI reconstruction, developing unsupervised and supervised deep learning-based reconstruction methods
  • Tensor Completion: Researching the application of tensor low-rank priors in medical image reconstruction
  • Deep Learning: Developing interpretable deep learning models, combining traditional optimization methods with neural networks
  • Compressed Sensing: Researching the application of compressed sensing theory in medical imaging

Education Background

  • Ph.D. Candidate - Harbin Institute of Technology (2021-Present)
  • Bachelor's Degree - Harbin Institute of Technology (2017-2021)

Professional Skills

  • Programming Languages: Python, Matlab, C++
  • Deep Learning Frameworks: PyTorch, TensorFlow
  • Operating Systems: Linux (Ubuntu), long-term use as main office and development system
  • Development Tools: Vibe Coding, Hermes Agent, Opencalw, Claude Code
  • Language Skills: English CET-6: 572, fluent reading of literature, good oral English, responsible for international student communication

Research Interests

My research focuses on medical image reconstruction, particularly dynamic MRI reconstruction. I am committed to developing deep learning methods based on tensor low-rank priors to improve the quality and speed of MRI reconstruction. My research combines traditional optimization methods with modern deep learning techniques to address practical problems in medical imaging.

Personal Profile

As a Ph.D. candidate, I not only focus on academic research but also actively participate in open-source projects and tool development. I have a deep understanding of modern AI tools and development processes, and can proficiently use various AI-assisted programming tools to improve development efficiency. I believe that the power of technology can promote the development of medical imaging and provide better support for clinical diagnosis.
